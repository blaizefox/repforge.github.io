About RepForge

A simple website project designed around workout tracking and organization.

THE IDEA

Simple Workout Tracking

RepForge is designed to give users a straightforward way to record their workouts. The tracker focuses on a few important pieces of information: the training day, exercise, sets, reps, and weight.

Keeping these details together can make a workout routine easier to organize and review.

RepForge Includes

  • Workout tracking
  • Workout history
  • Exercise information
  • Progress examples
  • Responsive design
