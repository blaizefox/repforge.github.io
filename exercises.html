EXERCISE GUIDE

Exercises

Use these common exercises as examples when planning and recording workouts.

Upper Body

Exercises that focus on the chest, shoulders, back, and arms.

  • Bench Press
  • Shoulder Press
  • Lat Pulldown
  • Bicep Curl
  • Tricep Extension

Lower Body

Exercises that focus on the legs and lower-body strength.

  • Squat
  • Leg Press
  • Romanian Deadlift
  • Lunge
  • Calf Raise

Core

Exercises commonly used to strengthen the muscles around the torso.

  • Plank
  • Crunch
  • Leg Raise
  • Russian Twist
  • Dead Bug
